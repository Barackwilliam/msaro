About Us

Personal Guiding, Not a Travel Agency

Just the Two of Us
No middlemen, no call centers

Msaro Guide Tanzania is the partnership of two Arusha-based professionals — Mussa Paulo Mussa, a certified Mountain & Safari Guide, and Amani Simon, our Assistant Guide and Hospitality Specialist. Together we plan, book and personally lead every Kilimanjaro climb, Meru ascent, Udzungwa trek and wildlife safari we offer.

You will never be handed off to a stranger on the day of your trip. The guide who plans your itinerary is the same guide who walks beside you on the mountain or drives you through the park — safe, authentic, and unforgettable, from the first message to the last night in camp.
