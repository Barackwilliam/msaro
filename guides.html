Get Ready

What to Pack for Your Kilimanjaro Climb

Everything you should bring — and a few things worth double-checking — before you meet Mussa at the trailhead.

Clothing & Layers
  • Waterproof, warm jacket (down/fleece) for summit night
  • Moisture-wicking base layers (avoid cotton)
  • Warm hat, gloves & buff/scarf
  • Broken-in hiking boots + camp sandals
  • Hiking socks (wool blend, several pairs)
  • Sunglasses with UV protection
Gear & Equipment
  • Daypack (25–35L) with rain cover
  • Sleeping bag rated to at least -10°C
  • Trekking poles
  • Headlamp + spare batteries
  • Water bottles or hydration bladder (min. 3L capacity)
  • Water purification tablets/drops
Health & Documents
  • Passport (valid 6+ months) & Tanzania visa
  • Travel/medical insurance with high-altitude evacuation cover
  • Yellow fever certificate (if arriving from an endemic country)
  • Personal medication + basic first-aid kit
  • Altitude sickness medication — discuss with your doctor beforehand
  • Sunscreen (SPF 30+) & lip balm with SPF
Personal Essentials
  • Cash (small USD/TZS notes) for tips and extras
  • Power bank & charging cables
  • Energy snacks/electrolyte sachets
  • Wet wipes & hand sanitizer
  • Camera or phone with extra storage
  • Small towel & toiletries

Not sure what else to bring for your specific route or season? Message Mussa directly and he'll send a full checklist for your trip.
