Tanzania Wildlife Safaris

Tanzania Wildlife Safaris: Serengeti, Ngorongoro & Tarangire

Go on a private Tanzania wildlife safari with a certified local guide from Arusha. Track the Big Five across the endless plains of the Serengeti, descend into the wildlife-packed Ngorongoro Crater, see elephant herds in Tarangire and tree-climbing lions at Lake Manyara, or follow the Great Wildebeest Migration. Choose a 4-day or 7-day northern circuit safari, or let us tailor one around your dates.

4 Days
4-Day Wildlife Safari

A fast-paced introduction to northern Tanzania’s big-name parks: elephant herds and ancient baobabs in Tarangire, flamingos and tree-climbing lions at Lake Manyara, the endless plains of the Serengeti, and a full day exploring the wildlife-packed floor of Ngorongoro Crater in search of the Big Five.

7 Days
7-Day Wildlife Safari

Our full northern circuit, giving each park the time it deserves — more days in the Serengeti to follow the game, a full crater day in Ngorongoro, and an extension to the otherworldly landscape of Lake Natron beneath the active volcano Ol Doinyo Lengai, where flamingos gather by the thousands.

Half / Full Day
Mikumi National Park

Tanzania’s fourth-largest national park is often called a "mini-Serengeti" for its wide-open Mkata floodplain, where lion prides, elephant herds, giraffes, zebras and buffalo graze in full view right beside the main road. It’s one of the most accessible parks in the country, making it a great option for a shorter safari or a scenic stop if you’re short on time but still want a genuine Big-Five-country experience, with over 400 bird species for keen birders too.

Half / Full Day
Lake Manyara National Park

Tucked beneath the dramatic wall of the Great Rift Valley escarpment, Lake Manyara is famous for its tree-climbing lions and the thousands of flamingos that turn its alkaline shoreline pink. The park packs remarkable variety into a small area — dense groundwater forest alive with blue monkeys and baboons, open grassland herds of wildebeest and buffalo, and the shimmering lake itself. Compact and scenic, it’s a perfect standalone day trip from Arusha or an easy add-on to a longer northern circuit safari.

Priced by season & group size

Run in partnership with our trusted Kenya-based safari guide, who takes over at the border to keep every leg of the trip properly licensed and locally led.

Price on request — permit-dependent

A once-in-a-lifetime trek to see mountain gorillas in the wild in Rwanda's Volcanoes National Park, arranged through our trusted Rwanda-based partner guide who handles permits and leads every trek in full compliance with strict park regulations. A powerful add-on to any Tanzania safari or Kilimanjaro climb, for travelers who want to see both East Africa's plains wildlife and its mountain gorillas in one trip.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the best Tanzania safari for first-timers?

The 4-day safari covering Tarangire, Lake Manyara, the Serengeti and Ngorongoro Crater is a great introduction. If you have more time, the 7-day safari gives each park the time it deserves.

Can I see the Big Five in Ngorongoro Crater?

Ngorongoro Crater is one of the best places in Africa to see the Big Five in a single day, including the rare black rhino.

When is the best time for a Serengeti safari?

The Serengeti is excellent all year. June to October is the classic dry season for game viewing, and January to February is calving season in the southern Serengeti.

Can I combine a safari with a Kilimanjaro climb?

Yes. Many travellers climb Kilimanjaro or Mount Meru first and then relax on safari. Mussa can plan the full trip from Arusha.

4-Day Wildlife Safari — Full Itinerary
Full Day-by-Day Itinerary
1
Arusha → Tarangire National Park
Drive + Game DriveFull DayElephantsBaobabs
Depart Arusha in the morning for the drive to Tarangire, a park famous above all for its enormous elephant herds — some of the largest concentrations found anywhere in Africa — gathering along the life-giving Tarangire River during the dry season. The landscape itself is unforgettable, dotted with ancient, swollen baobab trees that can live for well over a thousand years, giving the park an almost prehistoric feel. Your afternoon game drive follows the riverbanks where elephant, giraffe, zebra, and often lion prides can be seen against this dramatic backdrop, before settling into your camp or lodge for the night.
2
Tarangire → Lake Manyara National Park
Game Drive + TransferFull DayTree-Climbing LionsFlamingos
A final morning game drive in Tarangire offers one more chance at its famous elephant herds before you head onward to Lake Manyara, tucked dramatically beneath the wall of the Great Rift Valley. Manyara is famous for its tree-climbing lions — a genuinely unusual behavior rarely seen elsewhere in Africa — and for the thousands of flamingos that often turn its alkaline shoreline pink. Your afternoon game drive winds through dense groundwater forest alive with blue monkeys and baboons before opening onto the lake's grassy plains, home to wildebeest, buffalo and giraffe.
3
Lake Manyara → Serengeti National Park
Scenic DriveFull DayNgorongoro HighlandsGame Viewing
Today's drive climbs up and over the dramatic Ngorongoro Highlands, with sweeping views down into the crater from the rim road, before descending onto the seemingly endless Serengeti plains — a landscape that genuinely lives up to the name, which means “endless plains” in the Maasai language. Wildlife density noticeably increases as you enter the park, with game viewing along the drive itself often turning up lion, elephant, and large herds of plains game well before you've even reached camp. You'll settle in for two nights at the heart of one of the world's most famous wildlife ecosystems.
4
Full-Day Serengeti Game Drive → Ngorongoro Crater → Return to Arusha
Game Drive + CraterFull DayBig FiveCrater Floor
An early game drive across the Serengeti's endless plains offers your best chance at predators — lion, cheetah and leopard are all present year-round — and, depending on the season, you may encounter the legendary wildebeest and zebra herds of the Great Migration thundering across the grassland. From there, you'll descend into Ngorongoro Crater, a UNESCO World Heritage Site and one of the highest densities of wildlife found anywhere on Earth within its 600m-deep, 20km-wide caldera walls, for a final unforgettable game drive in search of the Big Five before the drive back to Arusha to conclude your safari.

7-Day Wildlife Safari — Full Itinerary
Full Day-by-Day Itinerary
1
Arusha → Tarangire National Park
Drive + Game DriveFull DayElephantsBaobabs
Depart Arusha for Tarangire, home to some of Africa's largest elephant herds and an otherworldly landscape studded with ancient baobab trees. Your afternoon game drive follows the Tarangire River, the park's lifeline during the dry season, where elephant, giraffe, zebra and often resident lion prides gather in large numbers against a backdrop unlike anywhere else on the northern circuit.
2
Tarangire → Lake Manyara National Park
Game Drive + TransferFull DayTree-Climbing LionsHippo Pools
A final Tarangire game drive precedes the transfer to Lake Manyara, tucked beneath the dramatic Rift Valley escarpment. The afternoon brings tree-climbing lions, hippo pools, and flamingo-lined shores, all within a compact, scenic park that packs remarkable variety into a small area.
3
Lake Manyara → Serengeti National Park (Central/Seronera)
Scenic DriveFull DayNgorongoro HighlandsGame Viewing
The drive climbs across the Ngorongoro Highlands, with a first glimpse down into the crater from the rim road, before descending into the game-rich central Serengeti around Seronera — widely considered the park's best year-round game viewing area thanks to its permanent water sources. An afternoon game drive en route to camp often turns up lion, elephant and abundant plains game.
4
Full-Day Serengeti Game Drive
Game DriveFull DayPredatorsMigration
A full day dedicated entirely to following the game across the Serengeti's endless plains — genuinely one of the best wildlife days on the whole northern circuit. This extra day (compared to the 4-day itinerary) gives your guide real flexibility to track predator activity and, depending on the season, position you for the Great Migration's dramatic herds of wildebeest and zebra.
5
Serengeti → Ngorongoro Conservation Area
Game Drive + TransferFull DayCrater RimGame Viewing
A final morning game drive in the Serengeti precedes the journey to the Ngorongoro Highlands, arriving at your crater-rim camp in time for sweeping views down into the caldera at sunset — one of the most spectacular campsite views in all of Africa, with the crater floor sometimes visible far below through evening mist.
6
Full-Day Ngorongoro Crater → Lake Natron
Crater + DriveFull DayBig FiveFlamingo Lake
An early descent into Ngorongoro Crater delivers one of the highest densities of wildlife found anywhere on Earth within its walls, with excellent Big Five viewing on the flat, grassy crater floor. From there, you'll drive north into the increasingly dramatic, otherworldly landscape surrounding Lake Natron, beneath the active volcano Ol Doinyo Lengai — a stark contrast to the greener parks visited earlier in the trip.
7
Lake Natron → Return to Arusha
Explore + DriveFull DayFlamingo ColoniesOl Doinyo Lengai
A morning exploring Lake Natron's mineral-rich, blood-red shoreline, home to the vast majority of East Africa's lesser flamingo population, which breeds here in huge numbers found nowhere else in the region. It's a fittingly dramatic final stop before the drive back to Arusha, concluding a safari that has covered elephant-filled floodplains, tree-climbing lions, the endless Serengeti, the wildlife-packed Ngorongoro Crater, and the otherworldly volcanic landscape of Natron.

The drive itself is scenic, passing through changing landscapes as you approach the park boundary, with anticipation building as the open floodplain finally comes into view.
2

4
Afternoon Game Drive & Departure
Floodplain Drive2-3 hrsReturn Journey
A further game drive exploring different sections of the floodplain and surrounding woodland, often with different wildlife activity than the morning as animals move with the changing light. You'll depart in the afternoon back toward Arusha, Dar es Salaam, or your next destination, with a full day of genuine wildlife encounters behind you.

Lake Manyara National Park — Day Timeline
Full Day-by-Day Itinerary
1
Morning: Depart Arusha & Park Entry
Scenic Drive1.5-2 hrsRift Valley
Depart Arusha and drive south beneath the dramatic wall of the Great Rift Valley escarpment, a genuinely spectacular geological feature visible for much of the journey. Entering the park through dense groundwater forest immediately signals you're somewhere special — the canopy here is thick and alive with the calls of blue monkeys and baboons swinging through the branches.
2
Late Morning Game Drive
Forest to Grassland2-3 hrsTree-Climbing Lions
The trail winds from forest into open grassland, where you'll watch closely for Manyara's famous tree-climbing lions — a genuinely unusual behavior rarely observed elsewhere in Africa, with prides often draped across acacia branches to escape heat and biting insects. Wildebeest, buffalo and giraffe are commonly seen on the surrounding plains as the vegetation opens up.
3
Midday: Lakeshore & Picnic Lunch
Lakeshore45-60 minFlamingos
Reaching the alkaline lake shore, often lined with thousands of flamingos and other waterbirds feeding in the shallows, makes for a spectacular picnic lunch backdrop with sweeping views across the water toward the Rift Valley wall. The pink-tinged shoreline is one of Manyara's signature sights and a favorite photography stop.
4
Afternoon: Final Game Drive & Return
Game Drive2 hrsReturn to Arusha
One last loop through a different section of the park before the drive back to Arusha, or onward if you're combining Manyara with a longer northern circuit safari. Afternoon light often brings different wildlife activity than the morning, giving one final chance at standout sightings before departure.

Great Wildebeest Migration Safari — Full Itinerary
Full Day-by-Day Itinerary
1
Arusha → Central Serengeti
Drive + Game DriveFull DayGame Viewing
Depart Arusha for the long, scenic drive across the Ngorongoro Highlands into the Serengeti, with game viewing along the way as wildlife density steadily increases. Your guide tracks the latest migration reports before departure to position you in the right sector of the park for the season — the Serengeti ecosystem is vast, and the herds move in a predictable but shifting annual circuit.
2
Full-Day Migration Tracking
Game DriveFull DayMigration Herds
A full day following the wildebeest and zebra herds, numbering well over a million animals during peak season, across the Serengeti's endless plains. Depending on the time of year, this may mean the calving grounds of the southern Serengeti/Ndutu plains (Jan–Mar), the western corridor (Apr–Jun), or the dramatic river crossings further north (Jul–Oct) — your guide plans the route around where the action is.
3
River Crossing Watch (Seasonal)
Game DriveFull DayRiver Crossings
If timing and season align, a day spent patiently watching the Grumeti or Mara River crossings — one of nature's most dramatic spectacles, with crocodiles waiting in the shallows as thousands of wildebeest attempt the crossing. Even outside peak crossing weeks, this stretch of the Serengeti holds outstanding resident wildlife, including healthy predator populations following the herds.
4
Full-Day Serengeti Game Drive
Game DriveFull DayPredators
A further full day in the Serengeti, with flexibility built in to chase the best current wildlife activity — lion prides, cheetah on the open plains, and leopard in the riverine forest are all present alongside the migrating herds themselves.
5
Serengeti → Ngorongoro Crater → Return to Arusha
Crater + DriveFull DayBig Five
A final game drive before departing the Serengeti, followed by a descent into Ngorongoro Crater for concentrated Big Five viewing on the crater floor — one of the highest wildlife densities anywhere on Earth — before the drive back to Arusha to conclude the safari.

Masai Mara Safari — Full Itinerary
Full Day-by-Day Itinerary
1
Serengeti/Arusha → Cross Border → Masai Mara
Border CrossingFull DayKenya Transfer
Depart from northern Tanzania and cross into Kenya at the Isebania/Sirari border post, where you're met by our trusted Kenya-based partner guide who leads the Masai Mara portion of the trip — Tanzanian guide licenses don't extend across the border, so a seamless handover to a fully certified Kenyan guide keeps everything legal and properly run. By afternoon you're inside the Mara ecosystem, with an introductory game drive as you settle in.
2
Full-Day Masai Mara Game Drive
Game DriveFull DayBig Cats
A full day exploring the Masai Mara's open grasslands, famous for having one of the highest concentrations of lion, cheetah and leopard anywhere in Africa. The Mara's rolling plains and scattered acacia trees make for classic, unobstructed game viewing all day long.
3
Mara River & Mara Triangle
Game DriveFull DayMara River
A day focused on the Mara River and the Mara Triangle, prime territory for spotting hippo and crocodile pods, and — during migration season (roughly July to October) — a strong chance of witnessing the wildebeest herds crossing over from the Serengeti side in dramatic fashion.
4
Final Game Drive & Return Transfer
Game Drive + TransferFull DayBorder Return
A final morning game drive before the transfer back across the border into Tanzania, where your Msaro Guide Tanzania team picks up the trip again for the journey back to Arusha or onward to your next destination.

Gorilla Trekking, Rwanda — Full Itinerary
Full Day-by-Day Itinerary
1
Fly to Kigali → Transfer to Volcanoes National Park
Flight + TransferHalf DayVirunga Mountains
Fly into Kigali, Rwanda, where you're met by our trusted Rwanda-based partner guide — gorilla trekking permits and guiding are tightly regulated and require a Rwanda-licensed guide, so this handover keeps the trek fully compliant with park rules. The scenic drive to Musanze/Ruhengeri, gateway to Volcanoes National Park, passes through Rwanda's famous “thousand hills” countryside.
2
Gorilla Trekking Day
Trek2-6 hrs (variable)Mountain Gorillas
The main event: an early start with park rangers, tracking one of Volcanoes National Park's habituated mountain gorilla families through bamboo forest and volcanic slopes. Trek duration varies considerably depending on where the gorilla family is that day — anywhere from a couple of hours to a more demanding six-hour trek — but reaching the gorillas and spending a permitted hour in their presence is a genuinely life-changing wildlife encounter found in only a handful of places on Earth.
3
Kigali City Tour & Departure
City TourHalf DayDeparture
Transfer back to Kigali with time for an optional city tour, including the moving Kigali Genocide Memorial, before your onward flight. A fitting, reflective close to a trip built around one of Africa's most remarkable conservation stories.
